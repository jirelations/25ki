## Experiments

1. Try the same query with different models
2. Adjust the parameters (e.g., creativity & openness)
3. Try the same query with different roles/system prompts
4. What is the most accurate you can get?
5. Where do you hit the safety guardrails?
6. Can you break it (without breaking any laws)? e.g.,
    1. Hallucinations (questions it has little training data for or people would commonly answer incorrectly), e.g., obscure information you know
    2. Bias (you may need to set up a role)
    3. Nonsensical questions
    4. Recursive questions
